两种信道状态：全概率、贝叶斯与离散 SNRQuiz

Original question · 中英对照Mock Quiz A (self-written)

A wireless link alternates between two states. In the "good" state, which occurs with probability 0.7, the bit error probability is \(10^{-4}\). In the "bad" state, which occurs with probability 0.3, the bit error probability is \(10^{-2}\).

一条无线链路在两种状态之间切换。"好"状态出现的概率为 0.7，此时误比特率为 \(10^{-4}\)；"坏"状态出现的概率为 0.3，此时误比特率为 \(10^{-2}\)。

(a) Find the average bit error probability of the link. (3 marks)

(a) 求该链路的平均误比特率。（3 分）

(b) A bit is found to be in error. What is the probability that the link was in the bad state when this bit was sent? (3 marks)

(b) 发现某个比特出错了。发送这个比特时，链路处于"坏"状态的概率是多少？（3 分）

(c) The received SNR is 10 dB in the good state and 0 dB in the bad state. Find the mean and the variance of the SNR (in linear scale), and express the mean SNR in dB. (4 marks)

(c) 好状态下接收 SNR 为 10 dB，坏状态下为 0 dB。求 SNR（线性值）的均值和方差，并把平均 SNR 用 dB 表示。（4 分）

全概率贝叶斯（必背）离散期望与方差讲义第 1b 章 p.5–14
题意讲解：这道题到底要干嘛

场景题目在讲一件什么事

一条无线链路时好时坏：比如有时被遮挡、有时畅通。题目把它简化成两种状态，每种状态出现的概率和误比特率都已知。

这是第 1b 章概率复习的典型题：分情况讨论（全概率）、由结果反推原因（贝叶斯）、离散随机变量的期望和方差。

符号题目里每个量是什么

good / bad两种信道状态，概率分别是 0.7 和 0.3（加起来是 1）
\(10^{-4},\ 10^{-2}\)各状态下的误比特率，也就是条件概率 P(出错 | 状态)
10 dB / 0 dB各状态下的接收 SNR（dB），算均值方差前要换成线性值 10 和 1

要求每一问在问什么

  • (a) 不知道当时处于哪个状态，一个比特出错的总概率是多少：两种情况各自算，再按出现概率加权。
  • (b) 已经知道"这个比特出错了"，反过来问"当时是坏状态"的概率：先有结果、再问原因，就是贝叶斯。
  • (c) 把 SNR 看成一个离散随机变量（10 的概率 0.7，1 的概率 0.3），求它的均值和方差，再把均值换回 dB。

注意读题容易误会的地方

  • (b) 的答案不是 0.3：0.3 是"事先"处于坏状态的概率，题目问的是"知道出错以后"的概率。
  • (c) 不能直接平均 dB 值：dB 是对数，先换成线性值再平均。

目的这道题想让你明白什么

全概率（分情况加权）和贝叶斯（由果推因）是第 1b 章的两个核心工具；还要会求离散分布的期望和方差，并注意 dB 和线性值的换算。

一句话：两状态信道：(a) 用全概率求平均误码率，(b) 用贝叶斯求"出错时处于坏状态"的概率，(c) 求 SNR 的均值和方差（先换线性值）。

思路：(a) 分情况讨论再加权，就是全概率；(b) 已经看到结果（出错），反过来问原因（处于哪个状态），就是贝叶斯；(c) 离散分布求期望和方差，先把 dB 换成线性值。

  1. (a) 全概率：\(P_e=P(G)\,P(e\mid G)+P(B)\,P(e\mid B)=0.7\times10^{-4}+0.3\times10^{-2}=7\times10^{-5}+3\times10^{-3}\)
    P_e = 3.07 × 10⁻³
  2. (b) 贝叶斯：\(P(B\mid e)=\dfrac{P(B)\,P(e\mid B)}{P_e}=\dfrac{3\times10^{-3}}{3.07\times10^{-3}}=0.977\)。分子就是 (a) 里"坏状态"那一项，分母就是 (a) 的答案。
  3. (c) 先换成线性值：10 dB → 10，0 dB → 1。
    E[γ] = 0.7 × 10 + 0.3 × 1 = 7.3　　E[γ²] = 0.7 × 100 + 0.3 × 1 = 70.3
  4. \(\mathrm{Var}(\gamma)=E[\gamma^2]-(E[\gamma])^2=70.3-53.29=17.01\)（标准差 4.12）；平均 SNR \(=10\log_{10}7.3=8.63\) dB。
(a) 3.07 × 10⁻³；(b) 0.977；(c) E[γ] = 7.3（8.63 dB），Var(γ) = 17.0

◆ Key points
What it tests
Total probability, Bayes' rule (blue box, memorise), mean and variance of a discrete random variable (Ch 1b p.5–14, Quiz scope).
Background
P(B) = ΣP(Ai)P(B | Ai); P(Ai | B) = P(Ai)P(B | Ai)/P(B); E[X] = Σx·p(x); Var(X) = E[X²] − (E[X])².
Method
Make a table of state, probability and conditional probability. (a) Weighted sum; (b) the matching term of (a) divided by the total of (a); (c) convert to linear, then E[X] and E[X²] from the table.
Common mistakes
Averaging dB values (7 dB, wrong); forgetting to subtract the squared mean; the Bayes denominator is the total error probability from total probability; total probability must sum over all states (a past mistake the lecturer pointed out).

Variations
A three-state channel works the same with one more row; or "probability that two consecutive bits are both wrong" (multiply if independent).

K为什么是 dB，不是 dBm

线性式 \(P_r=P_t\cdot K\cdot(d_0/d)^{\gamma}\) 里，\(P_r\)、\(P_t\) 是功率（mW），K 和 \((d_0/d)^{\gamma}\) 是乘在功率上的倍数，没有单位。K 的意思是"在参考距离 d₀ 处，接收功率是发射功率的多少倍"，所以是比值，取对数后是 dB。\(K_{dB}=-31.5\) dB 表示 1 m 处信号衰减到发射功率的 \(10^{-3.15}\approx1/1400\)。

取 dB 后乘法变加法，只有 \(P_t\) 带 dBm，其余都是 dB，结果自然是 dBm：

\[\underbrace{P_{r,dBm}}_{\text{dBm}}=\underbrace{P_{t,dBm}}_{\text{dBm}}+\underbrace{K_{dB}}_{\text{dB}}-\underbrace{10\gamma\log_{10}(d/d_0)}_{\text{dB}}\]
运算结果意思
dBm + dBdBm功率乘一个倍数，还是功率
dBm − dBmdB两个功率相除得倍数（余量、SNR）
dB + dBdB倍数乘倍数，还是倍数
dBm + dBm没有意义功率乘功率不是功率

反过来验证：如果 K 也是 dBm，右边就成了 dBm + dBm，相当于"功率 × 功率"，没有物理意义。

Taking dB turns products into sums; only \(P_t\) is in dBm and everything else is in dB, so the result is in dBm:

\[\underbrace{P_{r,dBm}}_{\text{dBm}}=\underbrace{P_{t,dBm}}_{\text{dBm}}+\underbrace{K_{dB}}_{\text{dB}}-\underbrace{10\gamma\log_{10}(d/d_0)}_{\text{dB}}\]

(a)平均时延和 rms 时延扩展是什么

基站发一个很短的脉冲，手机收到的是先后到达的几份"回声"：0 µs 到的最强（0 dB），1 µs 到的弱一点（−3 dB），3 µs 到的最弱（−6 dB）。这张表就是功率时延谱。平均时延 μ 是回声能量的"重心"在哪个时刻；rms 时延扩展 σ 是能量在时间上散得多开。真正重要的是 σ，它决定回声会不会拖进下一个符号。

为什么要除以 1.752：这是按功率加权的平均，加权平均要除以权重之和。就像按学分算平均成绩，要除以总学分。三条路径的功率（线性值）是 1、0.501、0.251，加起来 1.752。

换个角度：除以 1.752 就是把功率变成"能量占比"：1/1.752 = 0.571，0.501/1.752 = 0.286，0.251/1.752 = 0.143，加起来是 1，相当于一个离散概率分布。于是 μ = 0 × 0.571 + 1 × 0.286 + 3 × 0.143 = 0.716 µs，σ 就是这个分布的标准差。这和第 1b 章离散随机变量的期望、方差是同一套算法。

  • 权重必须用线性值，dB 值（0、−3、−6）是负数，不能当权重。
  • σ 不是"最大时延 − 最小时延"：这里时延差 3 µs，σ 只有 1.03 µs，因为晚到的回声功率小、权重低。

(b)最大多普勒频移和相干时间

(a)What mean delay and rms delay spread are

A very short pulse arrives as several echoes: the strongest at 0 µs (0 dB), a weaker one at 1 µs (−3 dB), the weakest at 3 µs (−6 dB). That table is the power delay profile. The mean delay μ is the "centre of gravity" of the echo energy in time; the rms delay spread σ is how widely the energy is spread. σ is what matters: it decides whether echoes leak into the next symbol.

Why divide by 1.752: this is a power-weighted average, and a weighted average is divided by the sum of the weights, just like a credit-weighted grade average is divided by the total credits. The linear powers are 1, 0.501 and 0.251, which sum to 1.752.

Equivalently, dividing by 1.752 turns powers into energy fractions: 1/1.752 = 0.571, 0.501/1.752 = 0.286, 0.251/1.752 = 0.143, which sum to 1 like a discrete probability distribution. Then μ = 0 × 0.571 + 1 × 0.286 + 3 × 0.143 = 0.716 µs and σ is the standard deviation of that distribution: the same mean and variance as for a discrete random variable in Ch 1b.

  • The weights must be linear; the dB values (0, −3, −6) are negative and cannot be weights.
  • σ is not "largest delay − smallest delay": the delays span 3 µs but σ is only 1.03 µs, because late echoes carry little power.

(b)Maximum Doppler shift and coherence time

Maximum Doppler shift fm: a moving receiver sees a slightly shifted frequency (like a siren rising in pitch as an ambulance approaches). The shift is \(f_d=\frac{v}{c}f_c\cos\theta\), largest when moving straight along the signal direction (cos θ = 1): \(f_m=vf_c/c\). Here v = 72 km/h ÷ 3.6 = 20 m/s and \(f_m=20\times1.8\times10^9/3\times10^8=120\) Hz.

Coherence time TC: how long the channel stays roughly constant. A larger Doppler makes the echo phases change faster, so the channel changes faster and TC is shorter: \(T_C\approx1/f_m=1/120=8.33\) ms. They are reciprocals: faster movement, larger fm, shorter TC; with no movement, fm = 0 and the channel does not change.

  • Divide km/h by 3.6 to get m/s; convert GHz to Hz; put TC and Ts in the same unit before comparing.

第 1 步为什么包络是瑞利分布

手机收到很多条相位随机的回波：\(r_I=\sum\alpha_n\cos\phi_n\)，\(r_Q=\sum\alpha_n\sin\phi_n\)。每个分量都是大量随机项之和，由中心极限定理近似为均值 0、方差 σ² 的独立高斯。包络是这个二维向量的长度 \(z=\sqrt{r_I^2+r_Q^2}\)，两个独立零均值高斯的"长度"服从瑞利分布：\(p(z)=\frac{z}{\sigma^2}e^{-z^2/(2\sigma^2)},\ z\ge0\)。

第 2 步从包络到 SNR

最后一步用了 \(z_0^2/(2\sigma^2)=\gamma_0N/(\bar\gamma N)=\gamma_0/\bar\gamma\)。

Step by step: where the Rayleigh outage 1 − e−γ₀/γ̄ comes from (not examined, for understanding)

The lecturer said the Rayleigh derivation will not be asked and the Quiz has no integration; in the exam, remember the result and use it. This only shows where it comes from.

IdeaMultipath → Rayleigh → exponential → integrate

Multipath sum → Gaussian I and Q components → Rayleigh envelope z → exponential power z² (and SNR γ) → integrating the exponential from 0 to γ₀ gives the outage probability.

Step 1Why the envelope is Rayleigh

The phone receives many echoes with random phases: \(r_I=\sum\alpha_n\cos\phi_n\), \(r_Q=\sum\alpha_n\sin\phi_n\). Each is a sum of many random terms, so by the central limit theorem they are approximately independent zero-mean Gaussians with variance σ². The envelope is the length of this 2-D vector, \(z=\sqrt{r_I^2+r_Q^2}\), and the length of two independent zero-mean Gaussians is Rayleigh: \(p(z)=\frac{z}{\sigma^2}e^{-z^2/(2\sigma^2)},\ z\ge0\).

Step 2From envelope to SNR

Power is amplitude squared: received power z², mean power E[z²] = 2σ². SNR is proportional to power: \(\gamma=z^2/N\), mean \(\bar\gamma=2\sigma^2/N\).

Step 3Integrate for the outage

"γ < γ₀" is the same as "z < z₀" with \(z_0^2=\gamma_0N\). Substitute \(u=z^2/(2\sigma^2)\), \(du=z\,dz/\sigma^2\):

\[P_{out}=\int_0^{z_0}\frac{z}{\sigma^2}e^{-z^2/(2\sigma^2)}dz=\int_0^{z_0^2/(2\sigma^2)}e^{-u}du=1-e^{-z_0^2/(2\sigma^2)}=1-e^{-\gamma_0/\bar\gamma}\]

The last step uses \(z_0^2/(2\sigma^2)=\gamma_0N/(\bar\gamma N)=\gamma_0/\bar\gamma\).

Other wayThe two formulas to memorise are a pair

Differentiating \(1-e^{-\gamma_0/\bar\gamma}\) with respect to γ₀ gives the exponential SNR distribution \(p(\gamma)=\frac{1}{\bar\gamma}e^{-\gamma/\bar\gamma}\); conversely \(\int_0^{\gamma_0}\frac{1}{\bar\gamma}e^{-\gamma/\bar\gamma}d\gamma=1-e^{-\gamma_0/\bar\gamma}\). The exponential distribution is the PDF and the outage probability is its CDF: remember one and you get the other.

问题注水法回答什么

Step by step: what water-filling is and why it allocates power this way

QuestionWhat water-filling answers

Given several parallel subchannels of different quality and a fixed total power, how should the power be split to maximise the total capacity? The transmitter must know the channel (informed transmitter).

AnalogyPouring water into an uneven pool

Each subchannel is one cell of a pool whose floor height is 1/γj (γj is its SNR with all the power): the better the channel, the lower the floor. Pour the total power in like water; it settles to a flat surface at 1/γ₀. Each cell gets power = surface − floor: good channels get more, poor ones less, and a cell whose floor is above the surface gets none.

WhyThis split is optimal

For d < dc the direct and reflected waves alternately add and cancel, so the power rises and falls with distance and the d⁻⁴ formula does not apply; only beyond dc does it fall steadily as d⁻⁴. This is the n = 2 (free space) and n = 4 (two-ray, far) of Part A Ch 5, and one reason why γ in the simplified model usually lies between 2 and 4.
◆ Key points
What it tests
The two-ray model: critical distance dc = 4hthr/λ and Pr ∝ (hthr)²/d⁴ far away (Ch 2 p.5). Not asked directly in the practices or past papers.

Common mistakes

Intuition
Far away the ground reflection is almost opposite in phase to the direct wave and cancels most of it, leaving a small difference, so the power falls faster than in free space; raising the antenna increases the path difference and recovers some power.
