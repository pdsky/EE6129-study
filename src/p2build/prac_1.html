题意讲解：这道题到底要干嘛

场景题目在讲一件什么事

有人拿着接收机，在离发射机 5 m、25 m、65 m、110 m、400 m、1000 m 六个地方各测了一次"收到的功率比发出的功率小多少"，表里的 \((P_r/P_t)_{dB}\) 就是这个比值（负数 = 损耗）。

题目给了一个路径损耗模型 \(P_r=P_tK(1/d)^{\gamma}\)，要你用这 6 个实测点把模型里的两个未知参数定下来，再用它算中断概率。

取 dB 以后模型变成一条直线：\((P_r/P_t)_{dB}=K_{dB}-\gamma\cdot10\log_{10}d\)。所以 (a) 本质上就是"给 6 个点，画一条最贴合的直线"。

要求每一问在问什么

  • (a) 用最小二乘（least squares）找最贴合 6 个点的直线，读出 K（dB）和 γ。
  • (b) "只考虑路径损耗时，接收功率比不中断所需的最低功率高 10 dB"：直线给出的平均功率比门限高 10 dB，即余量 10 dB。阴影把功率往下压超过 10 dB 就中断。题目没给阴影的标准差 σ，要从 (a) 的拟合残差里算：σ² = 均方误差（MSE）。

注意读题容易误会的地方

  • 横坐标用 10 log₁₀d，不是 log₁₀d，否则 γ 会差 10 倍。
  • "10 dB above" 是余量，不是门限本身；和 P2 Q2 的 Δ 是同一个意思，中断概率 = Q(余量/σ)。
  • MSE 是方差 σ²，代进 Q 之前要开根号。

目的这道题想让你明白什么

实测数据怎么变成模型：直线部分是路径损耗（平均趋势），点在直线周围的散布是阴影（随机波动），散布的大小直接决定中断概率。

知识讲解 + 详细解析：最小二乘拟合 K、γ，再求阴影中断

知识讲解路径损耗 + 阴影 = 一条直线 + 随机散布

简化路径损耗模型（simplified path loss model，讲义第 2 章 p.8）：\(P_r=P_tK(d_0/d)^{\gamma}\)，d₀ = 1 m。K 是 1 m 处的功率增益（由环境、天线决定的常数），γ 是路径损耗指数（path loss exponent）。老师课上强调：这是专有名词，线性和 dB 两种形式都要背，考试不给。

两边取 10 log₁₀ 变成 dB：\(M=(P_r/P_t)_{dB}=K_{dB}-\gamma\cdot10\log_{10}(d/d_0)\)。令 \(x=10\log_{10}d\)，它就是一条直线 \(M=K_{dB}-\gamma x\)：截距是 KdB，斜率是 −γ。

实测点不会正好落在直线上，偏离的部分就是阴影（shadowing）\(\psi_{dB}\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_\psi^2)\)（p.11–12）。讲义 p.13 的做法：找让"实测 − 模型"的均方误差（MSE）最小的直线，并且 MSE = E[Xi²] = σψ²。所以一组数据同时给出两样东西：直线（平均趋势）和点的散布（阴影方差）。

中断概率（outage probability，p.14–15）：\(P_{out}=P(\psi_{dB}\lt-x)=Q(x/\sigma_\psi)\)，x = 不含阴影时的平均接收功率 − 最低要求功率，也就是余量（margin）。

详细解析(a) 用两个正规方程求 K 和 γ

  1. 做什么：写出误差平方和 \(F(K,\gamma)=\sum_i(M_i-K+\gamma x_i)^2\)。为什么：最小二乘就是让 F 最小，老师课上就是这样写的。
  2. 对 K 求偏导令其为 0：\(\sum_i(M_i-K+\gamma x_i)=0\) → \(K=\bar M+\gamma\bar x\)。意思是最佳直线一定经过平均点 (x̄, M̄)。
  3. 对 γ 求偏导令其为 0：\(\sum_i x_i(M_i-K+\gamma x_i)=0\)，把上一式代入整理，得 \(\gamma=-S_{xM}/S_{xx}\)。
  4. 怎么算：计算器上先求四个和，再算两个 S（这样比逐点算 x − x̄ 少出错）：
xi = 10 log₁₀di：6.99、13.98、18.13、20.41、26.02、30.00
Σx = 115.53，Σx² = 2566.75，ΣM = −640，ΣxM = −13671.57
Sxx = Σx² − (Σx)²/6 = 2566.75 − 2224.64 = 342.11
SxM = ΣxM − ΣxΣM/6 = −13671.57 + 12323.49 = −1348.08
γ = 1348.08 / 342.11 = 3.94
KdB = M̄ + γx̄ = −106.67 + 3.94 × 19.26 = −30.8 dB

详细解析(b) 残差 → σ → Q 函数

  1. 残差 ei = 实测 − 预测（解答里的表已逐点列出）：−1.67、+5.88、−2.77、−3.77、+3.32、−0.99。它们加起来是 0，这是最小二乘的性质，正好用来检查。
  2. 阴影方差：σψ² = Σe²/6。为什么除以 6 而不是 5：讲义定义 MSE = E[Xi²]，就是简单平均，考试照讲义除以 n。
  3. 余量："只算路径损耗时比最低功率高 10 dB" → x = 10 dB。中断 = 阴影把功率往下拉超过 10 dB：\(P_{out}=P(\psi_{dB}\lt-10)=Q(10/\sigma_\psi)\)。

σψ = √11.87 = 3.45 dB

自检三个快速检查

  • 直线经过平均点 (19.26, −106.67)；残差之和 = 0。
  • γ = 3.94 落在讲义 p.9 表里城市宏蜂窝的 3.7–6.5 范围内，合理。
  • 余量 10 dB ≈ 2.9σ，中断约千分之二。对比：余量 2.33σ 才对应 1%，量级对得上。

官方答案为什么和标准最小二乘对不上

官方答案是 K = −31.4 dB、γ = 3.86、Pout = 6.19 × 10⁻⁴。把官方的 K、γ 代回 6 个点，MSE = 13.1，比最小二乘的 11.87 还大，所以它不是这组数据的最小二乘解。(b) 用的是老师课上的 MSE = 9.656：10/√9.656 = 3.22，查表取 3.23 得 6.19 × 10⁻⁴。老师说结果"在 −31.4、3.86 附近就可以"。考试照上面的方法写清每一步、用自己算出的数即可。

易错常见丢分点

  • 横坐标用 log₁₀d 而不是 10 log₁₀d：γ 会变成 39.4，明显不合理。
  • 斜率是 −γ：算出斜率 −3.94，所以 γ = +3.94，别写成负的。
  • d 用米代入（d₀ = 1 m）。老师说今年 Quiz 有人把 2 km 写成 log 2。
  • MSE 是方差，进 Q 之前要开根号；写成 Q(10/11.87) 是错的。
  • 余量 10 dB 直接用 dB，不要换成线性：这个中断公式本来就是 dB 形式推出来的（老师课上特别提醒）。

考试英文写法

(a) In dB the model is a straight line, \(M=K_{dB}-\gamma x\), with \(x=10\log_{10}(d/d_0)\) and \(d_0\) = 1 m. Least squares minimises \(F(K,\gamma)=\sum_i(M_i-K_{dB}+\gamma x_i)^2\). Setting \(\partial F/\partial K=0\) and \(\partial F/\partial\gamma=0\) gives \(K_{dB}=\bar M+\gamma\bar x\) and \(\gamma=-S_{xM}/S_{xx}\).

With Σx = 115.53, Σx² = 2566.75, ΣM = −640 and ΣxM = −13671.57: Sxx = 342.11, SxM = −1348.08, so γ = 3.94 and KdB = −106.67 + 3.94 × 19.26 = −30.8 dB.

(b) The shadowing variance equals the MSE of the fit: σψ² = (1/6)Σei² = 71.23/6 = 11.87 dB², σψ = 3.45 dB. Outage occurs when shadowing lowers the power by more than the 10 dB margin: Pout = P(ψdB < −10) = Q(10/σψ) = Q(2.90) ≈ 1.85 × 10⁻³.

一句话：dB 下路径损耗是一条直线，最小二乘给出 K、γ，剩下的均方误差就是阴影方差 σ²；中断概率 = Q(余量 / σ)。

Concept + walkthrough: least-squares fit of K, γ, then shadowing outage

ConceptPath loss + shadowing = a straight line + random scatter

Simplified path loss model (lecture Ch 2 p.8): \(P_r=P_tK(d_0/d)^{\gamma}\), d₀ = 1 m. K is the power gain at 1 m (a constant set by the environment and antennas), and γ is the path loss exponent. The lecturer stressed in class that this is a fixed name, and that both the linear and the dB forms must be memorised; they are not given in the exam.

Take 10 log₁₀ of both sides to get dB: \(M=(P_r/P_t)_{dB}=K_{dB}-\gamma\cdot10\log_{10}(d/d_0)\). With \(x=10\log_{10}d\) this is a straight line \(M=K_{dB}-\gamma x\): the intercept is KdB and the slope is −γ.

The measured points never lie exactly on the line; the deviation is the shadowing \(\psi_{dB}\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_\psi^2)\) (p.11–12). The method on p.13: find the line that minimises the mean squared error (MSE) between measurement and model, and MSE = E[Xi²] = σψ². So one data set gives two things: the line (mean trend) and the scatter of the points (shadowing variance).

Outage probability (p.14–15): \(P_{out}=P(\psi_{dB}\lt-x)=Q(x/\sigma_\psi)\), where x = mean received power without shadowing − minimum required power, i.e. the margin.

知识讲解为什么阴影在 dB 上是高斯

阴影（shadowing）是信号沿途被很多障碍物（楼、树、墙）各衰减一次：总衰减 ψ = A₁A₂…AM，是很多因子的乘积（讲义第 2 章 p.11）。取对数后乘积变成和：log ψ = Σ log Ai。很多随机量相加，按中心极限定理趋向高斯，所以 ψdB 服从高斯，ψ 本身（线性值）就叫对数正态（log-normal）。

Concept + walkthrough: log-normal shadowing outage, forwards and backwards

ConceptWhy shadowing is Gaussian in dB

Shadowing means the signal is attenuated once by each of many obstacles on the way (buildings, trees, walls): the total attenuation ψ = A₁A₂…AM is a product of many factors (lecture Ch 2 p.11). Taking the log turns the product into a sum: log ψ = Σ log Ai. A sum of many random terms tends to a Gaussian (central limit theorem), so ψdB is Gaussian, and ψ itself (the linear value) is called log-normal.

Conclusion: as long as you work in dB (dBm), the received power is an ordinary Gaussian, \(P_r\sim\mathcal N(\mu_\psi,\sigma_\psi^2)\). μψ is the mean received power without shadowing (shadowing has zero mean, as the lecturer stressed), and σψ is the standard deviation of the shadowing, whose unit is really dB.

ConceptThe Q function and the lecturer's "rearrange" trick

The Q function (Ch 1b): \(Q(x)=P(N\gt x)\) with N ~ N(0, 1), i.e. the area of the right tail of the standard Gaussian. Q is decreasing: Q(0) = 0.5 and it gets smaller as x grows; by symmetry Q(−x) = 1 − Q(x).

The trick the lecturer wants you to master (p.14–15): rewrite "Pr < Pmin" as "random variable < −x", keeping the random variable on the left and moving everything else to the right, then use symmetry to write it as Q(x/σ).

知识讲解 + 详细解析：时延扩展与平坦衰落的速率上限

知识讲解功率时延谱、平均时延、rms 时延扩展

多径信道里，同一个发射脉冲经不同路径到达，最早那条记为 τ = 0，其余晚到 τ。功率时延谱（power delay profile）Pr(τ) 就是"晚到 τ 的那部分有多少功率"（讲义第 2 章 p.30）。

把 Pr(τ) 当成权重（除以总功率后就是一个概率分布），就能像求随机变量那样求两个数（p.30 的公式，考试会给，老师说不用背）：

  • 平均时延（mean delay spread）\(\mu_{T_m}=\int\tau P_r(\tau)d\tau\,/\int P_r(\tau)d\tau\)：回声"平均"晚到多久。
  • rms 时延扩展 \(\sigma_{T_m}=\sqrt{\int(\tau-\mu_{T_m})^2P_r(\tau)d\tau\,/\int P_r(\tau)d\tau}\)：回声在时间上"散得多开"，就是标准差。

为什么关心 σTm：回声拖得比符号还长，就会漏进下一个符号，造成码间干扰（ISI），这叫频率选择性衰落（p.32）；回声相对符号很短，整个信号带宽内衰落一样，叫平坦衰落（flat fading，p.33）。讲义的经验判据（蓝框，要背）：

条件结论
σTm/Ts < 0.1平坦衰落
σTm/Ts ≫ 1频率选择性衰落
介于两者之间（如 0.15）老师说：不能下结论

详细解析(a) 均匀时延谱的 μ 和 σ

  1. 分母（总功率）：\(\int_0^{10}1\,d\tau=10\)。为什么要除：Pr(τ) = 1 没有归一化，不除就不是加权平均。
  2. 平均时延：\(\int_0^{10}\tau\,d\tau=\tau^2/2\,\big|_0^{10}=50\)，μ = 50/10 = 5 µs。直觉：均匀分布左右对称，均值在正中间。
  3. rms 时延扩展，两种算法结果一样：
    按讲义定义：\(\int_0^{10}(\tau-5)^2d\tau=\big[(\tau-5)^3/3\big]_0^{10}=125/3+125/3=83.33\)，除以 10 得 8.33 µs²；
    或用 E[τ²] − μ²：\(\int_0^{10}\tau^2d\tau/10=(1000/3)/10=33.33\)，33.33 − 25 = 8.33 µs²。
  4. 开根号：σ = √8.33 = 2.89 µs。捷径：[0, a] 上均匀分布的标准差 = a/√12 = 10/3.46。
μTm = 50 / 10 = 5 µs
σTm² = 83.33 / 10 = 8.33 µs²
σTm = 2.89 µs

详细解析(b) 平坦衰落允许的最高符号速率

  1. 套判据：σTm/Ts < 0.1 → Ts > 10σTm。意思是回声最多只占一个符号的十分之一。
  2. 算 Ts：Ts > 10 × 2.887 = 28.87 µs。
  3. 换成速率：Rs = 1/Ts，符号越长速率越低，所以不等号翻过来。1/µs = 10⁶/s。
Ts > 28.87 µs
Rs < 1 / (28.87 × 10⁻⁶ s) = 34641 sym/s ≈ 34.6 ksps

合理性：1 MHz 的信号（Ts = 1 µs）σ/Ts = 2.89 ≫ 0.1，是频率选择性，需要均衡（第 6 章）或 OFDM（第 7 章）；所以平坦衰落只能用几十 ksps 量级，对得上。

易错常见丢分点

  • 以为 Pr(τ) = 1 已经归一化，分母写 1：μ 会变成 50 µs。
  • rms 时延扩展是标准差：8.33 要开根号。
  • 不等号方向：平坦衰落要求符号长、速率低，答案是上限 Rs < 34.6 ksps，不是"等于"。
  • 题目说的 mean delay spread 就是平均时延 μTm，不是 σ。

Concept + walkthrough: delay spread and the flat-fading rate limit

ConceptPower delay profile, mean delay, rms delay spread

In a multipath channel one transmitted pulse arrives over different paths; the first arrival is τ = 0 and the others arrive τ later. The power delay profile Pr(τ) says how much power arrives with delay τ (lecture Ch 2 p.30).

Treat Pr(τ) as a weight (divided by the total power it is a probability distribution) and compute two numbers just as for a random variable (formulas on p.30; they are given in the exam and the lecturer said they need not be memorised):

  • Mean delay (mean delay spread) \(\mu_{T_m}=\int\tau P_r(\tau)d\tau\,/\int P_r(\tau)d\tau\): how late the echoes arrive "on average".
  • rms delay spread \(\sigma_{T_m}=\sqrt{\int(\tau-\mu_{T_m})^2P_r(\tau)d\tau\,/\int P_r(\tau)d\tau}\): how widely the echoes are spread in time, i.e. the standard deviation.

Why σTm matters: if the echoes last longer than a symbol they leak into the next symbol and cause inter-symbol interference (ISI); this is frequency-selective fading (p.32). If the echoes are short compared with the symbol, the fading is the same across the whole signal bandwidth: flat fading (p.33). The rule of thumb on the slides (blue box, memorise it):

ConditionConclusion
σTm/Ts < 0.1flat fading
σTm/Ts ≫ 1frequency-selective fading
in between (e.g. 0.15)the lecturer: no conclusion can be drawn

Step by step(a) μ and σ of a uniform delay profile

  1. Denominator (total power): \(\int_0^{10}1\,d\tau=10\). Why divide: Pr(τ) = 1 is not normalised; without dividing it is not a weighted average.
  2. Mean delay: \(\int_0^{10}\tau\,d\tau=\tau^2/2\,\big|_0^{10}=50\), μ = 50/10 = 5 µs. Intuition: the uniform distribution is symmetric, so the mean is in the middle.
  3. rms delay spread, two ways with the same result:
    by the slide definition: \(\int_0^{10}(\tau-5)^2d\tau=\big[(\tau-5)^3/3\big]_0^{10}=125/3+125/3=83.33\), divided by 10 gives 8.33 µs²;
    or with E[τ²] − μ²: \(\int_0^{10}\tau^2d\tau/10=(1000/3)/10=33.33\), and 33.33 − 25 = 8.33 µs².
  4. Square root: σ = √8.33 = 2.89 µs. Shortcut: the standard deviation of a uniform distribution on [0, a] is a/√12 = 10/3.46.
μTm = 50 / 10 = 5 µs
σTm² = 83.33 / 10 = 8.33 µs²
σTm = 2.89 µs

Step by step(b) Highest symbol rate for flat fading

  1. Apply the rule: σTm/Ts < 0.1 → Ts > 10σTm. The echoes may occupy at most a tenth of a symbol.
  2. Compute Ts: Ts > 10 × 2.887 = 28.87 µs.
  3. Convert to a rate: Rs = 1/Ts; a longer symbol means a lower rate, so the inequality flips. 1/µs = 10⁶/s.
Ts > 28.87 µs
Rs < 1 / (28.87 × 10⁻⁶ s) = 34641 sym/s ≈ 34.6 ksps

Sanity check: a 1 MHz signal (Ts = 1 µs) has σ/Ts = 2.89 ≫ 0.1, so it is frequency-selective and needs equalisation (Ch 6) or OFDM (Ch 7); flat fading is only possible at rates of tens of ksps, which fits.

PitfallsWhere marks are usually lost

  • Assuming Pr(τ) = 1 is already normalised and using 1 as the denominator: μ would become 50 µs.
  • The rms delay spread is a standard deviation: take the square root of 8.33.
  • Direction of the inequality: flat fading needs long symbols and a low rate, so the answer is an upper limit Rs < 34.6 ksps, not an equality.
  • The "mean delay spread" in the question is the mean delay μTm, not σ.

◆ Key points
What it tests
Mean delay and rms delay spread from a power delay profile, and the flat / frequency-selective decision (Ch 2 p.29–33).
Background
Normalise P(τ) and treat it as a probability density; E[τ], E[τ²] and variance = E[τ²] − (E[τ])² (Ch 1b).
Method
① μ = ∫τP(τ)dτ / ∫P(τ)dτ; ② E[τ²] = ∫τ²P(τ)dτ / ∫P(τ)dτ; ③ σ = √(E[τ²] − μ²); ④ flat if σ/Ts < 0.1 → lower bound on Ts → upper bound on Rs.
Key formula
Uniform on [0, a]: μ = a/2, σ = a/√12. Here a = 10 µs → 5 µs and 2.89 µs.
Common mistakes
The denominator is ∫P = 10, not 1. Flat fading needs long symbols (low rate), so keep the inequality the right way round; 34.6 ksps is an upper limit.
Variations
"Is a 1 MHz signal (Ts = 1 µs) flat?" σ/Ts = 2.89 > 0.1 → frequency-selective, needs equalisation or OFDM. For a discrete profile (a few echoes) replace the integrals by weighted sums (Section 2.4 example on the learning page).
