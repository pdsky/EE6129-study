宏分集为什么能降低中断概率

Original question · 中英对照Practice 4

One common technique used to reduce outage probability is Macro-diversity, where the signal transmitted by a Mobile Station is received and processed by multiple Base Stations. Explain why this might reduce outage probability.

降低中断概率的一种常用技术是宏分集（Macro-diversity）：移动台发出的信号由多个基站同时接收和处理。解释为什么这样能降低中断概率。

宏分集阴影衰落讲义第 4 章 p.3–4
  1. 中断主要由阴影衰落（大尺度）造成：手机和某个基站之间被楼房、地形挡住，信号长时间偏低。
  2. 几个基站在不同位置，手机到它们的传播路径不同，被同一障碍物同时挡住的可能性小，各条链路的阴影近似独立。
  3. 宏分集选最强的基站（或合并多个基站的信号）。只有所有链路同时低于门限才会中断，独立时 \(P_{out}=\prod_iP_{out,i}\)，比单条链路小得多。
  4. 例：单链路中断 10%，两个独立基站同时中断只有 1%（Practice 2 第 2 题的 [Q(Δ/σ)]²）。链路相关时效果打折扣。
多个基站的阴影衰落近似独立，所有链路同时深衰落的概率 = 各自概率之积，远小于单一链路
官方答案：参见讲义（第 4 章 p.3–4）
◆ 本题知识点
考什么
宏分集的原理（论述题，第 4 章 p.3–6）。
答题要点
① 中断主要由阴影衰落造成（大尺度、变化慢，加功率不划算）；② 几个基站位置不同，传播路径不同，阴影近似独立；③ 选最强的基站或合并，只有所有链路同时变差才中断，Pout = ΠPout,i；④ 条件：链路相关时效果下降（p.21）。
区分
宏分集对付阴影，基站之间相距很远；微分集对付多径，天线只需相隔几个波长（p.4–5）。
数字例
单条链路中断 10%，两个独立基站同时中断只有 1%，三个只有 0.1%。
联系
Practice 2 第 2 题给出了定量公式：独立时 [Q(Δ/σ)]²，相关时要先条件化再积分。
写作提示
论述题按"原因 → 机制 → 公式 → 条件"四句写，每句一个要点，比长段落更容易拿分。
  1. Outage is mainly caused by shadowing (large-scale fading): buildings or terrain block the path between the mobile and a base station, keeping the signal low for a long time.
  2. The base stations are at different locations, so the propagation paths differ and are unlikely to be blocked by the same obstacle; the shadowing on the different links is approximately independent.
  3. Macro-diversity selects the strongest base station (or combines several). Outage occurs only when all links are below the threshold; for independent links \(P_{out}=\prod_iP_{out,i}\), much smaller than for a single link.
  4. Example: a single link is in outage 10% of the time; two independent base stations are in outage together only 1% of the time ([Q(Δ/σ)]² in Practice 2 Q2). Correlated links reduce the benefit.
Shadowing on links to different base stations is roughly independent; the probability that all links are in a deep fade together is the product of the individual probabilities, far smaller than for one link
Official answer: see the lecture notes (Ch 4 p.3–4)
◆ Key points
What it tests
The principle of macro-diversity (essay question, Ch 4 p.3–6).
Answer outline
① Outage is mainly due to shadowing (large-scale, slowly varying; extra power is an inefficient fix); ② base stations at different locations have different propagation paths, so the shadowing is roughly independent; ③ selecting the strongest base station or combining means outage only when all links are poor, Pout = ΠPout,i; ④ condition: correlated links reduce the gain (p.21).
Do not confuse
Macro-diversity combats shadowing, with base stations far apart; micro-diversity combats multipath, with antennas only a few wavelengths apart (p.4–5).
Numbers
One link in outage 10%: two independent base stations 1%, three 0.1%.
Link
Practice 2 Q2 gives the quantitative formula: [Q(Δ/σ)]² when independent; when correlated, condition first and then integrate.
Writing tip
Write an essay answer as four sentences, "cause → mechanism → formula → condition", one point each. That earns marks more reliably than one long paragraph.
P4 · Q2

离散 SNR 分布下的 DPSK 与 MRC

Original question · 中英对照Practice 4

Consider a DPSK transmission system, where the transmit signal power and noise power are 1W each. The symbol error probability for DPSK is \(\tfrac12e^{-\gamma}\), where γ is the signal to noise ratio.

考虑一个 DPSK 传输系统，发射信号功率和噪声功率都是 1 W。DPSK 的误符号概率为 \(\tfrac12e^{-\gamma}\)，γ 为信噪比。

(a) If the SNR γ has a distribution given as, \(\gamma=\dfrac{\alpha P_T}{\sigma_n^2}\), \(\alpha=\begin{cases}8 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac13\\ 11 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac23\end{cases}\), where \(P_T\) and \(\alpha P_T\) are the transmitted and received signal powers respectively, and \(\sigma_n^2\) is the noise power. Find the average SNR \(\bar\gamma\) and average symbol error probability \(\bar P_s\).

(a) 若 SNR 为 \(\gamma=\dfrac{\alpha P_T}{\sigma_n^2}\)，其中 α 以概率 1/3 取 8、以概率 2/3 取 11；\(P_T\) 和 \(\alpha P_T\) 分别是发射和接收信号功率，\(\sigma_n^2\) 是噪声功率。求平均 SNR \(\bar\gamma\) 和平均误符号概率 \(\bar P_s\)。

(b) If the receiver employs a 2-branch MRC technique, and the SNR for each branch has the distribution given in part (a), find the output SNR \(\bar\gamma_\Sigma\), average symbol error probability \(\bar P_s\), array gain g and diversity gain d.

(b) 若接收机采用 2 支路 MRC，每条支路的 SNR 都服从 (a) 中的分布，求输出 SNR \(\bar\gamma_\Sigma\)、平均误符号概率 \(\bar P_s\)、阵列增益 g 和分集增益 d。

(c) The paths are now not independent. Specifically, \(\gamma_1=r_1+\beta\) and \(\gamma_2=r_2+\beta\), and the shadowing term β has the following distribution, \(\beta=\begin{cases}7 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac13\\ 10 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac23\end{cases}\), where β is independent of \(r_1\) and \(r_2\), and \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) have the following distribution, \(r_i=\begin{cases}0.6 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac13\\ 1.2 & \text{with a probability of }\tfrac23\end{cases}\); for i = 1, 2. Find \(\bar\gamma_\Sigma\), \(\bar P_{s,diversity}\), g and d.

(c) 现在两条路径不再独立：\(\gamma_1=r_1+\beta\)，\(\gamma_2=r_2+\beta\)。阴影项 β 以概率 1/3 取 7、以概率 2/3 取 10，且与 \(r_1\)、\(r_2\) 独立；\(r_i\)（i = 1, 2）以概率 1/3 取 0.6、以概率 2/3 取 1.2。求 \(\bar\gamma_\Sigma\)、\(\bar P_{s,diversity}\)、g 和 d。

离散分布求期望MRC：SNR 相加阵列增益 g / 分集增益 d相关削弱分集讲义第 4 章 p.16、p.21、p.27
  1. (a) \(P_T=\sigma_n^2=1\)，所以 γ = α。\(\bar\gamma=8\cdot\tfrac13+11\cdot\tfrac23=10\)。
    P̄s = ⅓·½e⁻⁸ + ⅔·½e⁻¹¹ = 5.59×10⁻⁵ + 0.56×10⁻⁵ = 6.15×10⁻⁵
  2. (b) MRC 输出 SNR = 两路之和。两路独立，组合：8+8 = 16（1/9），8+11 = 19（2 × 1/3 × 2/3 = 4/9），11+11 = 22（4/9）。
    γ̄_Σ = (16 + 76 + 88)/9 = 20
    P̄s = ½[e⁻¹⁶·1/9 + e⁻¹⁹·4/9 + e⁻²²·4/9] = 7.56×10⁻⁹
  3. \(g=\bar\gamma_\Sigma/\bar\gamma=20/10=2\)；\(d=-\log_{10}\bar P_s/\log_{10}\bar\gamma=8.12/1=8.1\)（γ̄ 用单路输入的 10）。
  4. (c) \(E[\gamma_i]=E[r_i]+E[\beta]=(0.2+0.8)+(7/3+20/3)=1+9=10\)，\(\bar\gamma_\Sigma=20\)，g = 2。
  5. \(\gamma_\Sigma=r_1+r_2+2\beta\)，三者独立，指数可以拆开：\(E[e^{-\gamma_\Sigma}]=E[e^{-r_1}]\,E[e^{-r_2}]\,E[e^{-2\beta}]\)。
    E[e^{−r}] = ⅓e^{−0.6} + ⅔e^{−1.2} = 0.1829 + 0.2008 = 0.3837
    E[e^{−2β}] = ⅓e^{−14} + ⅔e^{−20} = 2.786×10⁻⁷
    P̄s = ½ × 0.3837² × 2.786×10⁻⁷ = 2.05×10⁻⁸，d = 8 − log₁₀2.05 = 7.7
(a) γ̄ = 10，P̄s = 6.15×10⁻⁵；(b) γ̄Σ = 20，P̄s = 7.6×10⁻⁹，g = 2，d = 8.1；(c) γ̄Σ = 20，P̄s = 2.05×10⁻⁸，g = 2，d = 7.7
官方答案：完全一致 ✓
(b)(c) 平均 SNR 一样，(c) 误码率却高了 2.7 倍、d 从 8.1 降到 7.7：两路共享阴影 β，一起变差，分集被削弱（讲义 p.21）。
◆ 本题知识点
考什么
离散 SNR 分布下的平均 SNR、平均误码率；MRC；阵列增益 g、分集增益 d；相关支路（第 4 章 p.16、p.21、p.27–28）。
前置知识
离散期望 Σx·p；独立事件概率相乘；E[f(X)] ≠ f(E[X])；独立变量 E[g(X)h(Y)] = E[g(X)]E[h(Y)]。
解题套路
(a) 直接求期望；(b) 列 2 × 2 组合表 → 输出 SNR = 两路之和 → 按概率加权；(c) 把 e−γΣ 拆成独立因子的乘积，分别求期望。
关键公式
g = γ̄Σ/γ̄；d = −log₁₀P̄s / log₁₀γ̄（γ̄ 是合并器输入的平均 SNR）。
捷径
(b) 也可以不列表：两路独立，E[e−(γ₁+γ₂)] = (E[e−γ])²，而 (a) 已经算出 E[e−γ] = 2 × 6.15×10⁻⁵ = 1.23×10⁻⁴，所以 P̄s = ½ × (1.23×10⁻⁴)² = 7.56×10⁻⁹。
易错
8 + 11 有 (8, 11)、(11, 8) 两种，概率 4/9；e⁻¹⁶ 这类极小数要保留有效数字；d 用输入的 10，不是输出的 20；(c) 共享的 β 进指数是 2β。
结论
(b)(c) 平均 SNR 都是 20、g 都是 2，但相关让误码率高 2.7 倍、d 从 8.1 降到 7.7。
  1. (a) \(P_T=\sigma_n^2=1\), so γ = α. \(\bar\gamma=8\cdot\tfrac13+11\cdot\tfrac23=10\).
    P̄s = ⅓·½e⁻⁸ + ⅔·½e⁻¹¹ = 5.59×10⁻⁵ + 0.56×10⁻⁵ = 6.15×10⁻⁵
  2. (b) The MRC output SNR is the sum of the two branches. With independent branches the combinations are 8+8 = 16 (1/9), 8+11 = 19 (2 × 1/3 × 2/3 = 4/9), 11+11 = 22 (4/9).
    γ̄Σ = (16 + 76 + 88)/9 = 20
    P̄s = ½[e⁻¹⁶·1/9 + e⁻¹⁹·4/9 + e⁻²²·4/9] = 7.56×10⁻⁹
  3. \(g=\bar\gamma_\Sigma/\bar\gamma=20/10=2\); \(d=-\log_{10}\bar P_s/\log_{10}\bar\gamma=8.12/1=8.1\) (γ̄ is the per-branch input SNR, 10).
  4. (c) \(E[\gamma_i]=E[r_i]+E[\beta]=(0.2+0.8)+(7/3+20/3)=1+9=10\), \(\bar\gamma_\Sigma=20\), g = 2.
  5. \(\gamma_\Sigma=r_1+r_2+2\beta\); the three are independent, so the exponential factorises: \(E[e^{-\gamma_\Sigma}]=E[e^{-r_1}]\,E[e^{-r_2}]\,E[e^{-2\beta}]\).
    E[e−r] = ⅓e−0.6 + ⅔e−1.2 = 0.1829 + 0.2008 = 0.3837
    E[e−2β] = ⅓e−14 + ⅔e−20 = 2.786×10⁻⁷
    P̄s = ½ × 0.3837² × 2.786×10⁻⁷ = 2.05×10⁻⁸, d = 8 − log₁₀2.05 = 7.7
(a) γ̄ = 10, P̄s = 6.15×10⁻⁵; (b) γ̄Σ = 20, P̄s = 7.6×10⁻⁹, g = 2, d = 8.1; (c) γ̄Σ = 20, P̄s = 2.05×10⁻⁸, g = 2, d = 7.7
Official answer: identical ✓
(b) and (c) have the same mean SNR, yet (c) has a 2.7 times higher error rate and d drops from 8.1 to 7.7: the shared shadowing β makes both branches fade together, which weakens diversity (lecture p.21).
◆ Key points
What it tests
Mean SNR and mean error rate for a discrete SNR distribution; MRC; array gain g and diversity gain d; correlated branches (Ch 4 p.16, p.21, p.27–28).
Background
Discrete expectation Σx·p; independent events multiply; E[f(X)] ≠ f(E[X]); for independent variables E[g(X)h(Y)] = E[g(X)]E[h(Y)].
Method
(a) Take the expectation directly. (b) Tabulate the 2 × 2 combinations → output SNR = sum of the branches → weight by probability. (c) Split e−γΣ into a product of independent factors and average each.
Key formulas
g = γ̄Σ/γ̄; d = −log₁₀P̄s / log₁₀γ̄ (γ̄ is the mean SNR at the combiner input).
Shortcut
(b) needs no table: for independent branches E[e−(γ₁+γ₂)] = (E[e−γ])², and (a) already gives E[e−γ] = 2 × 6.15×10⁻⁵ = 1.23×10⁻⁴, so P̄s = ½ × (1.23×10⁻⁴)² = 7.56×10⁻⁹.
Common mistakes
8 + 11 occurs in two orders, (8, 11) and (11, 8), probability 4/9. Keep enough significant figures for tiny numbers like e⁻¹⁶. Use the input SNR 10 for d, not the output 20. In (c) the shared β enters the exponent as 2β.
Conclusion
In (b) and (c) the mean SNR is 20 and g = 2, but correlation raises the error rate 2.7 times and lowers d from 8.1 to 7.7.

高斯衰落因子下的 BPSK 与等增益合并

Original question · 中英对照Practice 4

A BPSK system uses M antennas to transmit a message {b} over a fading channel, where b is equally likely to be +1 or −1. The signal received from the kth transmit antenna can be expressed as \(y_k=\alpha_kb\sqrt E+w_k,\ k=1,2,\dots,M\), where E is the transmitted signal energy, \(\alpha_k\) is a real-value multiplicative dampening factor, and \(w_k\) is an additive white Gaussian noise with zero mean and a variance of \(\sigma_2^2\). The damping factor, \(\alpha_k\) can be modelled as a Gaussian process with mean \(\alpha_0\) and variance \(\sigma_1^2\). The receiver uses its output, z, as the decision variable. The decision rule is, \(\hat b=+1\) when z > 0, −1 when z ≤ 0.

一个 BPSK 系统用 M 根天线在衰落信道上发送消息 {b}，b 等概取 +1 或 −1。来自第 k 根发射天线的接收信号为 \(y_k=\alpha_kb\sqrt E+w_k,\ k=1,2,\dots,M\)，其中 E 是发射信号能量，\(\alpha_k\) 是实值的乘性衰减因子，\(w_k\) 是零均值、方差为 \(\sigma_2^2\) 的加性高斯白噪声。衰减因子 \(\alpha_k\) 可建模为均值 \(\alpha_0\)、方差 \(\sigma_1^2\) 的高斯过程。接收机用其输出 z 作为判决变量，判决规则为：z > 0 时 \(\hat b=+1\)，z ≤ 0 时 \(\hat b=-1\)。

(a) Derive an expression for the average bit error probability when M = 1.

(a) 推导 M = 1 时平均误比特概率的表达式。

(b) Derive the average bit error probability for the case when the receiver uses an equal weight combining scheme.

(b) 推导接收机采用等权合并（equal weight combining）时的平均误比特概率。

高斯随机变量之和等增益合并讲义第 4 章 p.19；第 1b 章 p.11
  1. (a) 设 b = +1（对称，另一种情况一样）。\(z=y=\alpha\sqrt E+w\)：两个独立高斯之和还是高斯。
    均值 α₀√E，方差 σ₁²E + σ₂²
  2. 出错 = z ≤ 0：\(P_b=P(z\le0)=Q\!\left(\dfrac{\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2}}\right)\)。
  3. (b) 等权相加：\(z=\sum_ky_k=b\sqrt E\sum_k\alpha_k+\sum_kw_k\)。各 \(\alpha_k\)、\(w_k\) 独立：
    均值 Mα₀√E，方差 M(σ₁²E + σ₂²)
  4. \(P_b=Q\!\left(\dfrac{M\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{M(\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2)}}\right)=Q\!\left(\dfrac{\sqrt M\,\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2}}\right)\)。
(a) Q(α₀√E / √(σ₁²E + σ₂²))；(b) Q(√M·α₀√E / √(σ₁²E + σ₂²))
官方答案：同上 ✓
均值按 M 增长、标准差按 √M 增长，所以 Q 函数的参数放大 √M 倍，这就是合并的好处。
◆ 本题知识点
考什么
衰落因子是高斯随机变量时的 BPSK 误码率；等权合并（第 4 章 p.19；第 1b 章独立高斯相加）。
前置知识
独立高斯之和仍是高斯，均值相加、方差相加；Var(cX) = c²Var(X)，所以 Var(α√E) = σ₁²E。
解题套路
设 b = +1（对称）→ 求判决变量 z 的均值和方差 → 出错 = z ≤ 0 → P = Q(均值 / 标准差)。
关键结论
M 路等权相加：均值 × M，方差 × M，标准差 × √M → Q 的参数放大 √M 倍。
易错
方差项 σ₁²E 别漏乘 E；标准差是 √(σ₁²E + σ₂²)，不是 σ₁√E + σ₂。
物理意义
即使没有噪声（σ₂ = 0），误码率也是 Q(α₀/σ₁) ≠ 0：衰落因子本身可能变成负数，把符号翻过来，这是衰落造成的"平台"；合并 M 路后变成 Q(√M α₀/σ₁)，平台降低。
举一反三
如果接收端知道每个 αk，可以按 αk 加权（MRC，p.15）；这里不知道 αk，所以只能等权。
  1. (a) Take b = +1 (the other case is symmetric). \(z=y=\alpha\sqrt E+w\): the sum of two independent Gaussians is Gaussian.
    mean α₀√E, variance σ₁²E + σ₂²
  2. Error = z ≤ 0: \(P_b=P(z\le0)=Q\!\left(\dfrac{\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2}}\right)\).
  3. (b) Equal-weight sum: \(z=\sum_ky_k=b\sqrt E\sum_k\alpha_k+\sum_kw_k\). All \(\alpha_k\) and \(w_k\) are independent:
    mean Mα₀√E, variance M(σ₁²E + σ₂²)
  4. \(P_b=Q\!\left(\dfrac{M\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{M(\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2)}}\right)=Q\!\left(\dfrac{\sqrt M\,\alpha_0\sqrt E}{\sqrt{\sigma_1^2E+\sigma_2^2}}\right)\).
(a) Q(α₀√E / √(σ₁²E + σ₂²)); (b) Q(√M·α₀√E / √(σ₁²E + σ₂²))
Official answer: same as above ✓
The mean grows like M while the standard deviation grows like √M, so the argument of Q increases by √M. That is the combining gain.
◆ Key points
What it tests
BPSK error rate when the fading factor is Gaussian; equal-weight combining (Ch 4 p.19; sums of independent Gaussians, Ch 1b).
Background
A sum of independent Gaussians is Gaussian with means and variances adding; Var(cX) = c²Var(X), so Var(α√E) = σ₁²E.
Method
Assume b = +1 (symmetric) → mean and variance of the decision variable z → error when z ≤ 0 → P = Q(mean / standard deviation).
Key result
Summing M branches with equal weights multiplies the mean by M and the variance by M, so the standard deviation grows by √M and the Q argument by √M.
Common mistakes
Do not forget the factor E in σ₁²E; the standard deviation is √(σ₁²E + σ₂²), not σ₁√E + σ₂.
Physical meaning
Even without noise (σ₂ = 0) the error rate is Q(α₀/σ₁) ≠ 0: the fading factor itself can become negative and flip the symbol, a fading-induced floor. Combining M branches lowers it to Q(√M α₀/σ₁).
Variation
If the receiver knew each αk it could weight by αk (MRC, p.15); here it does not, so equal weights are used.
