Q1(a) 蜂窝网络的组成

14 marks
Original question

Considering a typical cellular system with the service area partitioned into clusters with each cell having a BS serving the MS. There are four other basic components in a typical cellular network system besides the “MS” and the “BS”. Explain the functions of these four components and sketch the block diagram to show how all these components function as a cellular network system.

系统组成MTSO / MSC呼叫流程课件 Part A p.10–14

课件 p.11 的图一共标了 6 个 component。题目说"除了 MS 和 BS 之外的四个"，就是剩下的 4 个：

  1. Component 1 · Gateway to the wireline system（有线网关）：移动网通往 PSTN（固定电话网）和 Internet 的出口。手机打固话、上网都要从这里出去，它负责两边信令和格式的转换。
  2. Component 3 · Databases（数据库）：HLR 存用户签约资料和所在位置区，VLR 存当前在本区漫游用户的临时记录。被叫寻呼、漫游、切换都要先查数据库。
  3. Component 4 · Security mechanism（安全机制）：鉴权（AuC 确认是合法用户）、空口加密（防窃听）、设备识别（EIR 拦截被盗手机）。
  4. Component 5 · Air interface standard（空口标准）：MS 和 BS 之间的无线协议，比如 AMPS、GSM、IS-95。它规定频段、多址方式（FDMA/TDMA/CDMA）、调制、控制信道和业务信道，以及上行（reverse）和下行（forward）链路。

把它们连成一个系统的是 MTSO（Mobile Telecommunications Switching Office，也叫 MSC）。课件图里那条标着 MTSO 的线就是它。题目要求说明"how all these components function as a cellular network system"，所以画完框图，还要用 p.12–14 的呼叫流程把各部件串起来。

  1. 开机初始化：MS 扫描并锁定最强的控制信道，经 BS 和 MTSO 握手。这一步要查数据库、做鉴权，然后登记位置。
  2. 主叫：MS 在控制信道上发出被叫号码，BS 把请求转给 MTSO。
  3. 寻呼：MTSO 查 HLR/VLR 找到被叫所在位置区，让那里的 BS 发寻呼。被叫是固话时，则经 Gateway 接到 PSTN。
  4. 接通：MTSO 在两个小区各分配一条业务信道，双方调到这条信道开始通话。
  5. 切换：MS 走进新小区时，MTSO 把通话换到新 BS 的业务信道，通话不中断。
  • MTSO 不在"四个部件"里，但框图里必须有它，否则各部件没法连起来。
  • 只画图、不写功能，或只列名字、不讲怎么配合，都会丢分。14 分大致是 4 个部件各 2–2.5 分，图和流程说明占剩下的分。
课外补充 · 课件里没有这些名字

课件 p.11 只写了 "Databases" 和 "Security Mechanism"。上面提到的 HLR（归属位置寄存器）、VLR（拜访位置寄存器）、AuC（鉴权中心）、EIR（设备识别寄存器）、MSC，是 GSM 标准里对应部件的名字。答案里写上能显得具体，但不是课件要求的内容。

可以这样理解：HLR 像你的"户口本"，永远记着你是谁、开通了什么业务、现在大概在哪个区域；VLR 像酒店的"住客登记"，你漫游到哪里，那里的 VLR 就临时登记你；AuC 像"门卫查身份证"；EIR 像"失窃手机黑名单"。

Q1(b) Erlang B 与中继效率

14 marks
Original question

For a cellular system, suppose you selected the cluster size of Nc = 9, with a total number of available channels of 504. Assume that the average users place 2 calls of 15 minutes duration per hour for the given cellular system.

  1. Determine the carrier load per cell and the trunking efficiency for a 2% Grade of Service (GOS)?
  2. Calculate the number of users that can be accommodated for this cell at that given GOS.
  3. Compute the carried load and trunking efficiency if the cluster size is changed to Nc=7. Comment on your results.
每用户话务量 AuErlang B 查表carried loadtrunking efficiency课件 p.44, 115–124

这道题的 Nc = 9 那问，信道数刚好和课件 p.44 的 AMPS 例题一样，都是每小区 56 条。课件那页写的是 "the total traffic carried by 56 channels is 45.9 Er/cell"，可以直接拿来对照。

需要的三个概念：

  • 每用户话务量 Au = 每小时呼叫次数 × 每次时长（小时）。这里是 2 × 15/60 = 0.5 Erlang，相当于每人每小时有半小时在通话，数值很大。
  • Erlang B 表：已知信道数 M 和阻塞率 GoS，查出在这个 GoS 下最多能承载的话务量 A。题目说的 "carrier load" 应是 carried load 的笔误。课件 p.44 把查表得到的 A 直接叫作这些信道承载的话务量。严格定义是 C = A(1 − PB)（p.120），两种都写最稳妥。
  • 中继效率 ηt = 承载话务量 ÷ 信道数，也就是平均每条信道忙的比例。课件 p.124 的算法是 4.46 / 10 = 44.6%。
  1. 每小区信道数 M = 504 / 9 = 56
  2. 查表 M = 56、B = 2% → A = 45.9 E；严格的承载话务量 45.9 × 0.98 = 45.0 E
  3. 中继效率 ηt = 45.9 / 56 = 0.82（用 45.0 算是 0.80）
  4. 用户数 U = A / Au = 45.9 / 0.5 = 91.8 → 91 人（向下取整：多 0.8 个人就超出 2% 的阻塞率了）
  5. 换成 Nc = 7：M = 72 → A = 61.0 E → ηt = 0.85 → U = 122 人

Q2(a) 从带宽算到用户数

16 marks
Original question

A cellular system has a total bandwidth of 30 MHz. Each full duplex voice channel uses two 25 kHz simplex channels. The system uses a 9-cell reuse pattern and 0.75 MHz from the total bandwidth is allocated for control channels. The system service area consists of 50 cells. It is a blocked system with a call blocking probability of 2%. If the offered traffic of each user is 0.025 Erlang, calculate:

  1. the traffic load in each cell and the trunking efficiency,
  2. the total number of users in each cell and in the system, respectively,
  3. the number of users per channel in each cell and in the system, and
  4. the maximum number of users in service at any instant in the system.
双工信道带宽控制信道unique channels最大同时用户课件 p.133–134

这题和课件 p.133–134 的城市规划例题是同一个模板。先把带宽换成信道数，后面就是查表和除法。

  1. 一条双工信道 = 两条 25 kHz 单工信道 = 50 kHz，所以 总信道 = 30 MHz / 50 kHz = 600
  2. 控制信道 0.75 MHz / 50 kHz = 15 条，话音信道 600 − 15 = 585 条
  3. 9 小区复用，每小区 585 / 9 = 65 条
  4. (i) 查表 M = 65、B = 2% → A = 54.4 E/小区；ηt = 54.4 / 65 = 0.837
  5. (ii) 每小区用户 54.4 / 0.025 = 2176；全系统 2176 × 50 = 108,800
  6. (iii) 每小区每信道 2176 / 65 = 33.5；全系统按"不重复信道"算 108,800 / 585 = 186
  7. (iv) 任意时刻最多同时通话人数 = 全系统信道总数 65 × 50 = 3250，约占用户总数的 3%
课件 p.134 的口径：每信道用户数用"不重复信道"（一簇的信道总数 95 × 7 = 665）做分母；最大同时服务数用"每小区信道 × 小区数"（95 × 31）。本题照搬：585 和 65 × 50。
  • 15 条控制信道是全系统共用的，从总数里扣一次，不是每小区扣 15 条。
  • (iii) 全系统那问分母是 585，不是 3250。585 条信道在 50 个小区里被重复使用了约 5.6 次。
  • 50 个小区不是 9 的整数倍，不影响计算：每个小区都有 65 条信道。

Q2(b) SIR 与簇大小、路径损耗指数、扇区化

12 marks
Original question

Derive and establish the following relationship between Signal-to-Interference Ratio SIRdB and cluster size Nc.

SIRdB = 1.76 + 20 log10(Nc)

  1. Consider the Simplified SIR analysis where SIR = (1/6)Qn and Q is the co-channel reuse factor and there are six co-channel interferers in the first tier. You may assume a path loss exponent of n = 4 in your initial evaluation. Determine the SIRdB for Nc=7 and comment on your results.
  2. If the required minimum SIRdB is to be least 20 dB, derive the corresponding value of the path loss exponent n for this case.
  3. Using the new value of n from part (ii), determine Co-Channel interference SIR for the worst case omnidirectional as shown below.

    SIR = 1 / [2(Q − 1)−n + 2Q−n + 2(Q + 1)−n]

  4. Using the new value of n from part (ii), determine the SIR when using co-channel interference SIR with 120° sectoring. State and comment on your results when compared to part (iii).
Q = D/R = √(3N)简化模型 Qn/6最坏情况120° 扇区课件 p.84–91, 143–145

推导 1.76 + 20 log N

  1. 假设（课件 p.88）：只算第一层 6 个同频干扰；MS 在小区边缘，离本小区 BS 距离 R；6 个干扰 BS 离 MS 都约为 D；收到的功率与距离的 −n 次方成正比。
  2. S/I = R−n / (6 D−n) = (D/R)n / 6 = Qn / 6
  3. 代入 Q = √(3N) 和 n = 4：
    S/I = (√(3N))4 / 6 = 9N2 / 6 = 1.5 N2
  4. 取 dB：
    SIRdB = 10 log101.5 + 10 log10N2 = 1.76 + 20 log10N
    所以 1.76 dB 就是 N = 1 时的 SIR（课件 p.89 表第一行）。

四个小问

  1. (i) 1.76 + 20 log 7 = 1.76 + 16.90 = 18.66 dB。高于语音约需的 18 dB，所以 7 小区复用最常用；但简化模型把 6 个干扰都放在距离 D，偏乐观。
  2. (ii) 要求 Qn/6 ≥ 100，Q = √21 = 4.583：
    n = log10600 / log104.583 = 2.778 / 0.661 = 4.20
  3. (iii) n = 4.2 代入最坏情况公式：
    (Q−1)4.2 = 212.6, Q4.2 = 597.9, (Q+1)4.2 = 1370.0
    SIR = 1 / (2/212.6 + 2/597.9 + 2/1370.0) = 1 / 0.01421 = 70.4 → 18.5 dB
    没达到 20 dB：MS 在小区边缘时，有两个干扰源只隔 D − R。
  4. (iv) 120° 扇区：
    SIR = 1 / (Q−n + (Q+0.7)−n) = 1 / (1/597.9 + 1/1086.3) = 385.7 → 25.9 dB
    比 (iii) 高约 7.4 dB，满足 20 dB 且余量约 6 dB。

(i) 那句评论在说什么：简化算法 vs 最坏情况

课件对同一个系统给了三种算法，区别只在一个问题上：6 个干扰基站离手机有多远？（完整的零基础讲解，见 Part A 讲解长文第七章 7.2–7.3。）

  • 简化算法（p.88）：假设 6 个都在距离 D（课件原文 "equidistant, D"），S/I = Qn/6 → 18.66 dB。
  • Case 1（p.91）：手机站在小区边上，等于从自己基站往一边走了 R。于是 2 个干扰变近（D − R），2 个变远（D + R），2 个基本不变（D）→ 17.27 dB。
  • Case 2（p.91）："To be conservative"，往最坏处想，6 个都按最近的 D − R 算 → 14.39 dB。

为什么一近一远不能抵消？因为强度按距离的 −4 次方变化。取 R = 1、N = 7，则 D = 4.58：

(iv) 公式里的 D 和 D + 0.7R 是怎么来的？课件 p.145 直接给了这两个距离。我按上图的几何精确算了一遍：手机站在自己 1 号扇区最远的那个角上（离自己基站 R），能照到它的只有身后两个同频小区的 1 号扇区，距离分别是 5.29R 和 4.36R。D = √21 R = 4.58R，所以 5.29R = D + 0.71R，就是课件的 D + 0.7R；4.36R = D − 0.22R，课件近似成 D。

  • SIR 余量还可以换成更小的簇（比如 N = 4），把容量补回来，写上加分。

Q2(c) 推导复用距离 D 和簇大小 N

12 marks
Original question

Considering a cellular system with hexagonal cells, each with cell radius R. The service area is partitioned into clusters. Frequency is reused. Derive the distance D between the centers of two adjacent co-channel cells and the number of cells N in each cluster.

相邻小区中心距 √3Ri、j 坐标归一化距离 Dnorm大六边形面积课件 p.73–76
  1. 相邻小区中心距离（p.73）：六边形半径 R（中心到顶点，等于边长）。中心到一条边的垂直距离是 R cos30° = (√3/2)R，相邻两个小区中心隔着一条公共边，所以相距
    2R cos30° = √3 R
  2. 建 i、j 坐标（p.73–74）：把相邻中心的距离 √3R 当作 1 个单位（"归一化"）。以参考小区中心为原点 (0, 0)，i 轴和 j 轴分别穿过两条相邻的边、垂直于这两条边，两轴夹角 60°。这样每个小区中心都落在整数坐标 (i, j) 上：沿 i 轴走 i 格，再沿 j 轴方向走 j 格。
  3. 算归一化距离（p.75）：60° 斜坐标不能直接用勾股定理，先把"沿 j 轴走 j 格"拆成两个互相垂直的分量：沿 i 轴方向是 j sin30° = j/2，垂直于 i 轴方向是 j cos30° = (√3/2) j。于是：
    Dnorm2 = [j cos30°]2 + [i + j sin30°]2 = (3/4)j2 + i2 + ij + (1/4)j2 = i2 + ij + j2
  4. 乘回实际长度（p.75）：1 个单位是 √3R：
    D = √3 R · Dnorm = √3 R · √(i2 + ij + j2)
    例：i = 2、j = 1，Dnorm2 = 4 + 2 + 1 = 7，D = √3R × √7 = √21 R ≈ 4.58R。
  5. 求 N（p.76）：同频小区中心之间相距 D，就像普通小区中心之间相距 √3R。普通小区的半径是 √3R ÷ √3 = R；按同样的比例，每个同频小区"管"的那块区域是一个半径 D/√3 的大六边形，它的面积正好等于一个簇（N 个小区）。半径 r 的正六边形面积是 (3√3/2) r2，所以：
    N × (3√3/2) R2 = (3√3/2) (D/√3)2 → N = D2 / (3R2)
    把第 4 步的 D2 = 3R2(i2 + ij + j2) 代进去：
    N = i2 + ij + j2 = Dnorm2
  6. 结论（p.75）：N = Dnorm2，所以 D = √3R · √N：
    D = R√(3N)， Q = D/R = √(3N)
    N 只能取 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 12, 13 …（p.76–77）；例如 i = 2、j = 1 时 N = 7。
  • 相邻小区中心距离是 √3R，不是 R，也不是 2R。
  • i、j 两轴夹角是 60°，不能直接 D² = i² + j²。要先把 j 拆成 j/2 和 (√3/2)j 两个分量，展开时 (3/4)j² + (1/4)j² 合起来才是 j²。
  • 求 N 要写出"大六边形面积 = 簇面积"这一步和半径 D/√3 的理由，只写结论拿不到推导分。
课外补充 · 另一种算 D 的方法

也可以用余弦定理：两段位移长 i√3R 和 j√3R，在三角形里夹角是 120°，D² = (i√3R)² + (j√3R)² − 2(i√3R)(j√3R)cos120° = 3R²(i² + ij + j²)，结果一样。作业里写课件 p.75 的投影法更稳妥。

Q3(a) GSM 容量与频谱效率

20 marks
Original question

A Mobile Communications (GSM) system has a service area of 4,500 km2 with a cluster size of 7. Each cell has a coverage area of 7.5 km2. The total system bandwidth of 50 MHz is divided into 25 MHz for uplink and 25 MHz for downlink. Between the uplink and downlink, there is a frequency separation of 45 MHz. There are a total of 125 duplex radio frequency carriers. Each carrier has a bandwidth of 2 × 200 KHz to support 8 users by Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA) with a frame duration of 4.615 ms. Each time slot is 156.25 bits long including coded speech of 116 bits. During a busy hour, each user makes 2 calls on average and each call lasts 5 minutes. If the trunking efficiency is 0.85, determine:

Q3(b)

WiFi 7（IEEE 802.11be）的性能、规格及与 802.11a 的比较

WiFi 7 是 Wi-Fi Alliance 对 IEEE 802.11be "极高吞吐量"（Extremely High Throughput, EHT）修订案的命名。Wi-Fi Alliance 于 2024 年 1 月 8 日推出 Wi-Fi CERTIFIED 7 认证 [2]，IEEE Std 802.11be-2024 于 2025 年 7 月正式发布 [1]。它的目标是多 Gbit/s 的吞吐量，以及更低、更稳定的时延，用于 AR/VR、8K 视频、云游戏和工业物联网等场景。

主要规格：① 工作于 2.4、5、6 GHz 三个频段；② 6 GHz 频段支持 320 MHz 信道，是 WiFi 6 最大 160 MHz 的两倍；③ 4096-QAM，每符号 12 bit，比 1024-QAM 的峰值速率高 20%；④ 多链路操作（MLO），设备可同时在多个频段上收发，提高吞吐、降低时延、增强可靠性，是认证必选功能；⑤ 一个用户可分配多个资源单元（Multi-RU），配合前导码打孔，宽信道中某段受干扰时可跳过该段，其余带宽继续使用；⑥ 最多 8 个空间流，支持多用户 MIMO；⑦ 单条 320 MHz 链路的理论峰值约 23 Gbit/s [1][3]。

与 802.11a 比较：802.11a（1999 年）工作于 5 GHz，信道 20 MHz，采用 64 点 FFT 的 OFDM（52 个子载波，其中 48 个承载数据），最高 64-QAM，单天线，速率 6–54 Mbit/s；接入方式为 CSMA/CA，每次传输只服务一个站 [4][5]。WiFi 7 的信道宽 16 倍，每符号比特数是 2 倍（12 对 6），空间流多 8 倍，再加上 LDPC 编码和更长的 OFDM 符号降低了开销，峰值速率约高 400 倍。更关键的是多用户效率：OFDMA 和 MU-MIMO 让多个用户同时共享信道，MLO 跨频段聚合，适合用户密集的场景，而 802.11a 只能逐个轮流传输。

结论：WiFi 7 的提升来自更宽的频谱、更高阶的调制、更多空间流和多链路并发。实际吞吐受距离、干扰和各国 6 GHz 频谱政策限制，通常远低于理论峰值，且 6 GHz 的覆盖范围比 5 GHz 小。

Q1(a)

Besides the MS and the BS, a cellular network has four further basic components (slide 11):

  1. Gateway to the wireline system — connects the mobile network, through the MTSO, to the PSTN and the Internet, so that mobile users can reach fixed-line telephones and data networks. It performs the signalling and format conversion between the two networks.
  2. Databases — the Home Location Register (HLR) stores each subscriber's profile and current location area; the Visitor Location Register (VLR) holds temporary records of users roaming in its area. The MTSO consults them to route calls, page the called user and support roaming and handoff.
  3. Security mechanism — authenticates a user before service is given (Authentication Centre, AuC), encrypts voice and signalling over the radio link against eavesdropping, and blocks stolen or unauthorised equipment (Equipment Identity Register, EIR), protecting subscribers and the operator against fraud.
  4. Air interface standard — the radio protocol between MS and BS (e.g. AMPS, GSM, IS-95 CDMA). It specifies the frequency band, the multiple access scheme (FDMA/TDMA/CDMA), modulation, control and traffic channels, and the uplink (reverse) and downlink (forward) links.

Block diagram (below): the BSs are connected to the Mobile Telecommunications Switching Office (MTSO, or MSC), which switches calls between cells, uses the databases and security functions, and reaches the PSTN/Internet through the gateway. The MS communicates with the BS over the air interface.

How the components work together:

  1. Initialisation: the MS scans for and locks onto the strongest setup (control) channel; a handshake through the BS lets the MTSO identify and authenticate the user and register its location.
  2. Mobile-originated call: the MS sends the called number on the setup channel; the BS forwards the request to the MTSO.
  3. Paging: the MTSO looks up the called user in the databases and pages it through the BSs of its location area; a call to a fixed line is routed through the gateway to the PSTN.
  4. Call accepted: the MTSO sets up the circuit and assigns a traffic channel in each cell; both units tune to their channels.
  5. Handoff: when the MS moves into a neighbouring cell, the MTSO switches the call to a traffic channel of the new BS without interrupting it.

Wi-Fi 7 (IEEE 802.11be): performance, specification and comparison with IEEE 802.11a

Wi-Fi 7 is the Wi-Fi Alliance name for IEEE 802.11be, the “Extremely High Throughput” (EHT) amendment to IEEE 802.11. The Wi-Fi Alliance launched Wi-Fi CERTIFIED 7 on 8 January 2024 [2], and IEEE Std 802.11be-2024 was published in July 2025 [1]. Its aim is multi-gigabit throughput with lower and more predictable latency for applications such as AR/VR, 8K video, cloud gaming and industrial IoT.

Key specifications. (1) Operation in the 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands. (2) Channels up to 320 MHz in the 6 GHz band, double the 160 MHz of Wi-Fi 6. (3) 4096-QAM, carrying 12 bits per symbol and giving 20% higher peak rates than 1024-QAM. (4) Multi-Link Operation (MLO): a device transmits and receives simultaneously over several links in different bands, increasing throughput, reducing latency and improving reliability; it is mandatory for certification. (5) Multiple resource units per user and preamble puncturing: when part of a wide channel suffers interference, that part is skipped and the rest of the bandwidth is still used. (6) Up to 8 spatial streams with multi-user MIMO. (7) A theoretical peak PHY rate of about 23 Gbit/s on a single 320 MHz link [1][3].

Comparison with 802.11a. IEEE 802.11a (1999) operates in the 5 GHz band with 20 MHz channels, using OFDM with a 64-point FFT (52 subcarriers, 48 carrying data), modulation up to 64-QAM, a single antenna, and rates of 6–54 Mbit/s; access is by CSMA/CA, so each transmission serves one station [4][5]. Wi-Fi 7 uses 16 times the channel width, twice the bits per symbol (12 versus 6) and up to 8 times as many spatial streams, and LDPC coding with longer OFDM symbols reduces overhead, so its peak rate is roughly 400 times higher. Just as important is multi-user efficiency: OFDMA and MU-MIMO let many users share a channel at the same time and MLO aggregates bands, which suits dense deployments, whereas 802.11a can only serve stations one after another.

Conclusion. Wi-Fi 7's gains come from wider spectrum, higher-order modulation, more spatial streams and simultaneous multi-link operation. Real throughput is limited by distance, interference and national 6 GHz spectrum rules and is usually far below the theoretical peak; coverage at 6 GHz is also smaller than at 5 GHz.
